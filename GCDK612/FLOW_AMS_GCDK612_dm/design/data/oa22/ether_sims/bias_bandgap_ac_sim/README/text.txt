

Simulation: bias_bandgap_ac_sim


Purpose: Measure the AC open loop gain and phase of the bandgap for stability.
	 Additional test includes measuring bandgap dc output, bandgap
	 temperature sweep, and bandgap trimming.
	
Description:
	The bias_bandgap_ac_sim test bench consists of the Bias_bandgap, whose bias
currents are derived from the bg_rt_current_ref2 block, in addition with an external bias 
cicuit is setup to model additional parasitic capacitive loading on the bandgap output.
A Loop breaking block, loop breaker, is included with the 
test bench for AC analysis.

States: tt	

Verification: 
	AC analysis is useful for determining the stability of the bandgap.	
	This test looks at the open loop gain in conjunction with the phase of the bandgap to
	determine the phase margin of the block. This is done with instance I0,
	loop_breaker. Loop breaker consists of a series inductor, that appears
	as a DC short between the AC_IN and AC_OUT pins and as an open under AC
	conditions, thus breaking the loop while allowing the circuit to bias
	up correctly under dc conditions. One side of the loop is tied to AC
	ground via a cap. While an AC signal is injected on the other side of
	the inductor. The AC gain can be measured by taking the ratio of AC_OUT
	and AC_IN (i.e. dB20(VF("/AC_OUT")/VF("/AC_IN")) ). Also, the phase
	response can be found based on this ratio (i.e.
	phase(VF("/AC_OUT")/VF("/AC_IN"))).
		
		The bandgap dc output voltage measurement can be measured simply be
	running a dc analysis on the block and then printing the final voltage
	observed on the VBG pin.
		
		Similar to the dc output voltage measurement, the bandgap output,VBG,
	can be measured as a function of temperature. This may be useful in
	determing the overall error in the bangap as a function of temperature
	or for expressing this error as variation in terms of ppm/deg C. The dc
	analysis option is chosen in the spectre environment with the added
	option of temperature sweep specifying the temperature range of
	interest.

		The bias_bandgap_ac test bench allows for trim adjustement of the bandgap for compensation
	against process variation. Process variation, such as substrate bipolar, resistor, and mosfet variation 
	will shift the bandgap final output voltage and center. The BGTRIM<3:0> bus allows the 
	bandgap to be simulated across process and re-centered accordingly. 

Input Signals: AC_IN			AC signal input
	       BGTRIM<3:0>		Bandgap trim adjustment

Output Signals: AC_OUT			AC signal output
	        VBG			Bandgap output
