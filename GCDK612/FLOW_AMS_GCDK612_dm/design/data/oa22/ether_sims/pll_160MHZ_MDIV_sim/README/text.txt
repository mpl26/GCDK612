Simulation:     160MHZ_MDIV_sim 


Purpose:    
	Verify operability  of feedback dividers at highest frequency and at the
slowest corner.

States:         ss_640M 

Description:
	The 160MHZ_MDIV_sim test bench contains an instantian of the PLL
feedback divider block,PLL_160MHZ_MDIV. The PLL_160MHZ_MDIV input is driven 
by a 640Mhz ideal periodic source. Capacitance is placed at outputs, CLKFB
and CLK160, for loading. The simulation is meant to ensure that the feedback dividers operate and provide
a feedback clock at the highest VCO frequency. 

Verification:
	The 160MHZ_MDIV_sim is verified by running a transient analysis using
the spectre simulator in the analog artist environment.The output clocks of 
the feedback dividers are checked to make sure the have full swing and good 
slew rate.

Input Signals:  VCOCLK 

Output Signals: CLKFB, CLK160
