Simulation:     NDVR_sim 

Purpose:        AC simulation of the Charge Pump driver NDVR at different
corners. The simulation is meant to check the stability of the internal 
feedback loop

States:         ff, tt, ss 

Description:    The NDVR_sim testbench contains an instantiation of the 
PLL_NDVR block. An ideal voltage source connected to a poly resistor is 
used to derive  a poly resistor based bias current for the block. V2 is 
the AC input source. The combination of resistor R8 the capacitor C0 and 
the voltage controlled voltage source E0 break the feedback loop and 
provide dc bias. The NDVR takes the single-ended DN signal from the phase 
frequency detector, converts it to a differential signal and  level-shifts 
the signals to appropriate voltage level to drive the nmos switches in the 
charge pump.


Verification:   The Openloop gain and the phase of the loop are plotted. 
The expressions for the Openloop gain and the phase are stored in the 
artist states for convenience and are automatically plotted after the 
simulation completes. The phase margin and the gain bandwidth product are 
printed in the Analog Design Environment  window. These can be checked to 
make sure that the numbers are acceptable.

Input Signals:  FB_IN

Output Signals: FB_OUT
