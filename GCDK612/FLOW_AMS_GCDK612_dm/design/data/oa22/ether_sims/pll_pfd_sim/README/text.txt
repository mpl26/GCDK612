Simulation:     PFD_sim 

Purpose:       Verify pulse widths of the phase frequency detector output.
 

States:          tt 

Description:   The PFD_sim test bench contains an instantiation of the phase 
frequency dectector PLL_PFD. The PLL_PFD inputs are driven by a clock source 
having the same frequency but are out of phase. The resulting phase difference 
causes a periodic pulse to appear on either the UP or DN output of the block.
The pulse-width of the UP and DN signals is proportional to the phase difference
between the clocks REFCLK and FBCLK.

Verification:  The PFD_sim test bench is verfied by running a transient 
analysis. The UP and DN signals from the phase frequency detector can be
looked at to check for proper operation of the phase frequency detector.In
addition the minimum pulse-width of the UP and DN signals can be checked, 
which occurs when the phase difference between the REFCLK and FBCLK is zero. 
The minimum pulse-width pulses need to be full-swing and wide enough to 
prevent a dead-zone in the Charge Pump.

Input Signals:  VCOCLK, REFCLK

Output Signals: UP, DN
