Simulation:     PFDCP_pss_sim 

Purpose:        Transient, Periodic Steady State and Phase Noise simulation 
of the combination of the Phase Frequency Detector and the Charge Pump. The
simulation is meant to check the combined contribution of the Phase Frequency 
Detector and the Charge Pump to the internal phase noise of the PLL. 

States:         tt 

Description:    The PFDCP_pss_sim testbench contains instantiations of the
phase frequency detector (PLL_PFD), the charge pump (PLL_CP) and the loop
filter (PLL_160MHZ_LF). The phase frequency detector clock inputs, REFCLK 
and FBCLK are driven by vpulse voltage sources V3 and V4. An ideal voltage 
source connected to a poly resistor is used to derive a poly resistor based 
bias current for the block. The zero-volt voltage source V2 is used to monitor
the charge output from the charge pump.  The simulation is meant to check 
the combined contribution of the phase frequency detector and the charge 
pump to the internal phase noise of the PLL.

Verification:   Transient, Periodic Steady State and Phase Noise simulations
are run using spectre and spectreRF. The phase noise can be plotted from the 
Results->Direct Plot->Main Menu option in the Analog Design Environment 
window. The phase noise plot gives a measure of the combined contribution of 
the phase frequency detector and the charge pump to the internal phase noise 
and jitter of the PLL.
 

Input Signals:  REFCLK, VCOCLK

Output Signals: OUT
